A routine vet checkup in Pakistan typically costs PKR 1,000–3,000, with vaccinations, diagnostics, and surgery priced separately on top. Prices vary meaningfully by city and clinic, which makes budgeting for pet healthcare harder than it should be, since most clinics don't publish rates online.

This guide breaks down realistic 2026 pricing across the most common vet services in Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad, so you know roughly what to expect before you book.

## Routine Checkup Costs

| Service | Typical Cost (PKR) |
|---|---|
| Standard consultation | 1,000 – 3,000 |
| Follow-up visit (same issue) | 500 – 1,500 |
| Senior pet wellness exam | 1,500 – 3,500 |

Prices in Karachi and Lahore tend to sit at the higher end of these ranges compared to smaller cities, largely reflecting clinic overhead rather than quality of care.

## Vaccination Costs

Core vaccines protect against the most dangerous and common diseases, and skipping them almost always costs more later in treatment.

| Vaccine | Typical Cost per Shot (PKR) |
|---|---|
| Rabies | 800 – 1,800 |
| Distemper (dogs) | 1,000 – 2,000 |
| Parvovirus (dogs) | 1,000 – 2,000 |
| FVRCP combination (cats) | 1,200 – 2,200 |

A full first-year vaccination schedule for a puppy or kitten, including boosters, typically totals PKR 5,000–12,000. Our [guide to what happens during a vet visit](/blogs/understanding-vet-visits) explains which vaccines your pet needs and when.

## Diagnostic Test Costs

Diagnostics are where costs escalate quickly, and they're also where prices vary the most between clinics based on whether tests are run in-house or sent to an outside lab.

| Test | Typical Cost (PKR) |
|---|---|
| Basic fecal exam | 500 – 1,200 |
| Complete blood count (CBC) | 1,500 – 3,500 |
| Full blood panel (organ function) | 3,000 – 8,000 |
| X-ray (per view) | 2,000 – 5,000 |
| Urinalysis | 1,000 – 2,500 |

Ask upfront whether results come same-day or take longer, since outsourced lab work can add a day or two to diagnosis, which matters if your pet is unwell.

## Surgery and Procedure Costs

| Procedure | Typical Cost (PKR) |
|---|---|
| Spay/neuter (cat) | 3,000 – 8,000 |
| Spay/neuter (dog, by size) | 6,000 – 20,000 |
| Wound stitching (minor) | 2,000 – 6,000 |
| Dental cleaning | 4,000 – 12,000 |
| Complex surgery (fracture, tumor removal) | 15,000 – 60,000+ |

Surgical costs depend heavily on anesthesia type, post-op monitoring, and whether the clinic has proper sterile facilities, which is part of why [checking a clinic's equipment](/blogs/how-to-find-a-vet-near-you-pakistan) before booking surgery matters more than it does for a routine checkup.

## Emergency Visit Costs

Emergency and after-hours care in Pakistan carries a premium, often on top of whatever treatment is needed.

| Service | Typical Cost (PKR) |
|---|---|
| Emergency/after-hours consultation | 3,000 – 6,000 |
| Overnight observation | 2,000 – 5,000 per night |
| Emergency stabilization (severe cases) | 10,000 – 30,000+ |

Given how quickly emergency costs escalate, having a trusted, verified clinic identified before you need one, rather than searching mid-crisis, is worth the ten minutes it takes.

## Home Vet Visit Pricing

If clinic travel isn't practical, home-visit vets in Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad typically charge a call-out fee of PKR 1,500–4,000 on top of the service cost, which is often comparable to in-clinic pricing for routine care. ## Why Prices Vary So Much Between Clinics

- **Location**: Clinics in upscale neighborhoods of Karachi and Lahore generally charge more than those in smaller cities or older parts of town.
- **Equipment**: Clinics with in-house X-ray and lab facilities charge more per test but often save you the delay of outsourcing.
- **Vet experience**: Specialists and senior vets typically charge higher consultation fees than newer general practitioners.
- **Brand and marketing**: Some clinics charge a premium purely for a polished waiting room, which doesn't always correlate with better medical outcomes.

## How to Budget for Pet Healthcare in Pakistan

1. Set aside PKR 15,000–25,000 for a healthy adult pet's first year, covering checkups, vaccinations, and preventive care.
2. Budget separately for an emergency fund of at least PKR 20,000, since a single unexpected injury or illness can exceed routine annual costs.
3. Ask for a written estimate before agreeing to any procedure beyond a standard checkup.
4. Compare at least two clinics for anything non-urgent and above PKR 5,000, prices genuinely vary that much.

## Ways to Reduce Vet Costs Without Cutting Corners

- **Stay current on preventive care.** A PKR 1,500 vaccination is far cheaper than treating the disease it prevents, which can run into tens of thousands of rupees.
- **Bundle visits.** Combining a checkup, vaccination, and deworming into one appointment often saves on repeated consultation fees.
- **Ask about payment plans.** Larger clinics in Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad increasingly offer installment options for expensive procedures like surgery.
- **Get a second opinion for anything above PKR 15,000.** This isn't about distrust; prices for the same procedure can vary significantly between clinics.
- **Keep records organized.** Avoiding repeat diagnostic tests because a previous clinic's results weren't shared saves both money and time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does a basic vet checkup cost in Pakistan?

A standard consultation typically costs PKR 1,000–3,000, varying by city and clinic. Karachi and Lahore tend toward the higher end of that range.

### Is pet insurance available in Pakistan?

Pet insurance is not widely available in Pakistan yet, which makes an emergency savings fund the more practical way to prepare for unexpected vet costs.

### Why do X-rays cost so much more at some clinics than others?

Pricing depends on whether the clinic owns its own X-ray equipment or has to send pets to a partner facility. In-house equipment often costs more per scan but saves time.

### Do vets in Pakistan charge for a written estimate before surgery?

Most reputable clinics provide a verbal or written estimate for free before a procedure. If a clinic refuses to give any cost estimate upfront, treat that as a red flag.

### Are home vet visits cheaper than clinic visits?

Not usually, once the call-out fee is included, though the actual service cost is often similar. Home visits save time and travel rather than money. See our [home vet cost breakdown](/blogs/home-vet-visits-pakistan) for specifics.

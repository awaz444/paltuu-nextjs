## What Should I Feed My Cat?

The simple answer is that cats need a balanced diet rich in animal protein.

Cats are obligate carnivores, which means their bodies are designed to get most of their nutrition from meat-based sources. Unlike humans, cats cannot thrive on vegetarian diets.

A healthy cat diet should include:

- High-quality protein
- Healthy fats
- Vitamins and minerals
- Taurine
- Fresh water

If you're asking **what should I feed my cat**, the safest option is high-quality cat food formulated specifically for cats.

A balanced diet helps support:

- Healthy muscles
- Strong bones
- Better digestion
- Healthy skin and coat
- Strong immune function

Following a proper **cat nutrition guide** is one of the best investments you can make in your pet's future.

## Why Cat Nutrition Matters

Poor nutrition is one of the most common causes of health problems in domestic cats.

Feeding low-quality food can contribute to:

- Obesity
- Digestive issues
- Skin problems
- Hair loss
- Nutrient deficiencies
- Low energy levels

Good nutrition can reduce veterinary costs and help your cat enjoy a longer, healthier life.

When researching **what should I feed my cat**, always focus on nutritional quality rather than simply choosing the cheapest option available.

## Kitten Feeding Guide

Kittens have very different dietary requirements compared to adult cats.

A growing kitten needs additional calories and nutrients.

### Birth to 8 Weeks

Kittens should receive:

- Mother's milk
- Veterinarian-approved milk replacement formula

### 2 to 6 Months

This is a critical growth period.

Feed:

- High-quality kitten food
- 3–4 meals daily
- Fresh water

### 6 to 12 Months

Continue feeding kitten-specific food until adulthood.

A proper **kitten feeding guide** helps support healthy growth, brain development, and immune function.

> **Important:** Never feed cow's milk to kittens because many cannot digest lactose properly.

## Adult Cat Feeding Guide

Adult cats require maintenance nutrition.

Most adult cats do well with:

- Two meals daily
- Measured portions
- Consistent feeding times

When deciding **what should I feed my cat**, remember that portion control is just as important as food quality.

Overfeeding is a common mistake among cat owners.

## Cat Feeding Schedule

Another common question after **what should I feed my cat** is "how often should I feed my cat?"

### Kittens

- 3–4 meals daily

### Adult Cats

- 2 meals daily

### Senior Cats

- 2–3 smaller meals daily

Consistency helps support healthy digestion and prevents overeating.

A regular **cat feeding schedule** is one of the easiest ways to improve your pet's health.

## What a Home Vet Visit Actually Covers

Home-visit vets in Pakistan typically carry a portable kit covering:

- Routine physical exams and health checkups
- Core vaccinations (rabies, distemper, parvovirus, and more)
- Deworming and basic parasite prevention
- Nail trims and minor grooming-adjacent care
- Follow-up checks after a clinic procedure
- Basic wound cleaning and dressing changes
- Guidance on diet, weight, and behavior

For a healthy adult pet due for its annual shots, a home visit is often genuinely more convenient than a clinic trip, with no waiting room stress for anxious animals.

## What a Home Visit Still Can't Do

This is the part people underestimate. Sterilisation surgery travels well with the right setup, but some things genuinely need a building. No home visit — Paltuu's included — can:

- Take X-rays or run in-clinic diagnostic imaging
- Return most blood panels on the spot (samples can be drawn and sent to a lab, with results taking a day or more)
- Handle emergencies involving trauma, poisoning, or breathing difficulty safely
- Keep your pet under observation for several hours after treatment
- Perform complex or high-risk surgery beyond routine sterilisation

If your pet needs any of the above, go straight to a clinic rather than booking a home visit that will end in a referral anyway. Our guide on [how to find a vet near you in Pakistan](/blogs/how-to-find-a-vet-near-you-pakistan) covers how to evaluate clinics for exactly these situations.

## Home Vet vs. Clinic: A Quick Decision Guide

**Book a home visit if:**

- It's a checkup, vaccination, deworming, grooming, or a routine spay/neuter
- Your pet has severe anxiety or aggression triggered by travel or waiting rooms
- You have multiple pets needing the same care at once
- You have mobility constraints that make clinic visits difficult

**Go to a clinic if:**

- Your pet needs X-rays or same-day lab diagnostics
- It's an emergency involving trauma, difficulty breathing, or suspected poisoning
- The condition is unclear and may need equipment to diagnose properly
- Your pet needs to be monitored for several hours after treatment

If you're unsure which category your situation falls into, describing the symptoms on the phone is worth the two minutes. Paltuu's dispatcher will tell you honestly if a home visit isn't the right call.

## What Actually Happens During a Home Visit

A typical home vet appointment follows a predictable flow:

1. **Booking**: You submit the request with your pet's details and address. On Paltuu, a dispatcher claims it within minutes.
2. **Confirmation call**: The dispatcher calls to triage further, confirm the address, and agree the final price before anything is booked.
3. **Assignment**: A vet or groomer is assigned to your case and you get a confirmation with their name and the agreed price.
4. **Setup**: On arrival, the vet finds a calm, well-lit spot — often a dining table or a clear patch of floor — and lays out their kit.
5. **Exam and treatment**: The same physical checkup you'd get at a clinic, covering eyes, ears, teeth, heart, lungs, and abdomen, followed by the treatment itself.
6. **Wrap-up**: You get notes on findings, any follow-up needed, and an updated vaccination record — then a chance to rate the provider.

A routine visit usually takes 20–40 minutes, similar to a clinic appointment minus the travel and waiting room time. Sterilisation surgery takes longer.

## Booking Tips for a Smoother Home Visit

- Confirm the exact services covered before booking; not every provider offers the same list.
- Have your pet's previous vaccination record ready — a photo on your phone works.
- Keep your pet in a familiar, calm space rather than crowding around during the exam.
- Clear a well-lit surface in advance, especially for grooming or a procedure.
- Ask about payment upfront; most home vets in Pakistan settle in cash on the day.
- If your pet is reactive or anxious, mention it when booking so the vet can plan accordingly.
- Expect the possibility of an on-the-spot extra charge if the vet finds something that needs treating during the visit.

## Is It Safe to Trust a Home Vet's Credentials?

The same verification questions apply as with clinics: confirm the person is a licensed DVM, not an unqualified assistant making house calls.
